Introduction

I have spent the last six years crafting digital experiences at the intersection of technology and creativity. My journey in full-stack development has been marked by a particular fascination with backend engineering and the emerging possibilities of machine learning.

Through extensive collaboration with blockchain teams, I've developed custom smart contracts and NFT platforms that push the boundaries of decentralized technology. Each project has been an opportunity to explore how code can reshape our digital landscape.

As a tech generalist with a BFA in Design & Technology from Parsons (class of 2017), I find myself uniquely positioned at the crossroads of creative design and technical implementation. This dual perspective informs my work at Manic.agency, where I collaborate on open-source projects that challenge conventional boundaries.

Currently, my explorations lead me through the convergent paths of AI, web3, and immersive experiences — seeking those moments where technology transcends utility to become art.
